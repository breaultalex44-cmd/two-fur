# 🐺 Two-Fur | Frost & Aurora Edition

> **Pan-Canadian Winter Hub, Global Intelligence & Expanded Festivals Calendar**

A premium, high-performance calendar web application designed for mobile and desktop screens, featuring advanced pan-Canadian statutory holidays, world elections, lunar cycles, 15+ international festivals, and a dynamic event logging system ("Take a Tok").

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://breaultalex44-cmd.github.io/two-fur/](https://breaultalex44-cmd.github.io/two-fur/)

---

## ❄️ Frost & Aurora Design System

* Built with an immersive winter aesthetic featuring deep arctic night gradients, frosted glassmorphism panels (`backdrop-filter`), custom scrollbars, and fluid responsive layouts optimized for mobile devices.



---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 📅 1. Comprehensive Pan-Canadian & Global Intelligence

* **Canadian Statutory & Provincial Holidays:** Complete tracking across all provinces and territories from 2026 to 2027.


* **15+ New Worldwide Festivals:** Includes iconic global celebrations such as the *Venice Carnival, Rio Carnival, Holi Festival of Colors, Songkran, Oktoberfest, Diwali, and Dia de los Muertos*.


* **World Elections & Global Observances:** Track international electoral showdowns, UN awareness days, and cultural milestones.


* **Lunar Phase Integration:** Automatic calculation of moon phases (New Moon, First Quarter, Full Moon, Last Quarter) right on the calendar grid.



### 📝 2. "Take a Tok" Dynamic Event Creator

* **Advanced Logging System:** Quickly create and save custom personal tasks, meetings, or reminders directly to any calendar date.


* **Persistent Smart Reminder Badge:** Pin high-priority items with custom visual badges that stay reliably highlighted in the sidebar.


* **Local Persistence:** All custom logs are automatically saved to your browser's `localStorage` across sessions.



### 🔍 3. Instant Global Search

* Instantly search through holidays, festivals, custom logs, and descriptions with real-time dropdown filtering and quick calendar date jumping.
